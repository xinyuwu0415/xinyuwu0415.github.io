Short Bio

Xin Yu Wu is currently a Ph.D. candidate at Southeast University, State Key Laboratory of Millimeter-Waves, under the supervision of Prof. Zhi Hao Jiang. He received his bachelor’s degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University in 2019.

His research focuses on liquid crystal-based beam-scanning antennas, phased array antennas, reconfigurable antennas, and array synthesis theory. He has published more than 10 peer-reviewed journal/conference papers, including papers in Advanced Materials, Engineering, and IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation. He also serves as a reviewer for IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation and IEEE Antennas and Wireless Propagation Letters.

He was supported by the National Natural Science Foundation of China for Basic Research of Young Students (Ph.D. Candidates) (国家自然科学基金青年学生基础研究项目(博士研究生), 30wCNY). He was supported by the 2024 IEEE AP-S Fellowship.
