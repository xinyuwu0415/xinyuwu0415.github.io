Short Bio

Xin Yu Wu is currently a Ph.D. candidate at Southeast University, State Key Laboratory of Millimeter-Waves, supervised by Prof. Zhi Hao Jiang. He received his B.Eng. degree from Northwestern Polytechnical University in 2019. His research focuses on liquid crystal-based beam-scanning antennas, phased array antennas, reconfigurable antennas, and array synthesis theory.

He has published more than 10 peer-reviewed journal and conference papers in venues including Advanced Materials, Engineering, and IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation. He has also filed Chinese and U.S. patent applications and serves as a reviewer for IEEE TAP and IEEE AWPL.

Research Experience

2-D Liquid Crystal Phased Array for SatCom

Hybrid phase assignment strategy by joint regulation of LCPS and RF chips; 8×8 proof-of-concept design using subarray-level sequential rotation and miniaturized LC phase shifters.

01/2024 – Present

Dual-Linearly Polarized LCPA at Ka-band

Compact 1×8 subarray layout for improved space utilization, gain increase, and dual polarization. Demonstrated beam scanning within ±40°.

06/2022 – 06/2024

2-D Circularly Polarized LCRA in K-band

48×48 reflectarray based on reflection-type LCPS and CP unit cells, achieving CP beam scanning within ±40° and 31.8 dBic maximum gain.

07/2021 – 07/2022

Array Synthesis & Optimization

Generalized sequential rotation theory, top-flat radiation pattern synthesis, Gerchberg–Saxton algorithm, alternating projection method, and genetic-algorithm-based pixelized element design.
