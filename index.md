<h2>Short Bio</h2>
<p>
Xin Yu Wu is currently a Ph.D. candidate at <b>Southeast University</b>, State Key Laboratory of Millimeter-Waves, under the supervision of <b>Prof. Zhi Hao Jiang</b>. He received his bachelor’s degree from <b>Northwestern Polytechnical University</b> in 2019.
</p>
<p>
His research focuses on <b>liquid crystal-based beam-scanning antennas</b>, <b>phased array antennas</b>, <b>reconfigurable antennas</b>, and <b>array synthesis theory</b>. He has published more than 10 peer-reviewed journal/conference papers, including papers in <i>Advanced Materials</i>, <i>Engineering</i>, and <i>IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation</i>. He also serves as a reviewer for <i>IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation</i> and <i>IEEE Antennas and Wireless Propagation Letters</i>.
</p>
