## About Me

Xin Yu Wu is currently a Ph.D. candidate at Southeast University under the supervision of Prof. Zhi Hao Jiang. His research focuses on liquid crystal-based beam-scanning antennas, phased array antennas, reconfigurable antennas, and array synthesis theory.

He has published papers in journals including **Advanced Materials**, **Engineering**, and **IEEE Transactions on Antennas and Propagation**.
